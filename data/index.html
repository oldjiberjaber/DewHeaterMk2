💡 How the Adaptive Self-Learning Thermal Engine Operates
Phase 1

Steady-State Conductance (Kloss)

Whenever optics settle stably at target (±0.35°C), the engine measures the exact holding PWM power required per degree above ambient. This automatically accounts for changing wind speeds, shroud insulation, and ambient humidity without any manual calibration.

Phase 2

Descent Lead Anticipation (τlead)

Using Iterative Learning Control (ILC), the engine evaluates every cooling descent towards target. If the optics undershoot the setpoint, the lead horizon is lengthened to brake earlier; if descent is sluggish, lead is reduced to achieve perfect, critically damped arrival.

📘 Auto-Tuning Guidance & Configuration Recommendations
Principle

How sTune Auto-Tuning Works

The controller executes an open-loop step-response test using inflection-point parameter identification. After baseline settling, it energizes the dew heater at a steady output power, logs temperature rate-of-rise, and measures process gain (K), thermal dead-time (L), and time constant (τ) to derive optimal PID constants.

Recommended

Setting Guidelines

  • Test Power (%): 40% – 60% (default 50%). Provides a distinct heating slope without thermal overshoot or excessive battery draw.
  • Baseline Settle: 10s – 20s. Lets DS18B20 sensor readings stabilize at ambient before heating begins.
  • Test Duration: 60s for Guide Scopes & finders; 90s – 120s for larger refractors, reflectors, or SCT corrector plates.
Methods

Tuning Rules Explained

  • No Overshoot (Recommended): Smooth, critically damped approach without temperature oscillation. Best for astronomical imaging to prevent internal tube currents.
  • Ziegler-Nichols (ZN): Rapid setpoint tracking with ~10-20% transient overshoot. Suitable for high wind or thick dew bands.
  • Cohen-Coon: Ideal when the temperature probe has longer thermal transit lag from the heater wire.
  • Damped Oscillation: Fast convergence with quarter-decay damping.
Procedure

Best Practices & Safety

  1. Tune when optics are cool and near ambient in a calm environment.
  2. Select the target channel (CH0 or CH1) and click Start Auto-Tune.
  3. Once complete, compare the calculated gains against your previous settings.
  4. Click Accept & Save to Flash to permanently store values in non-volatile NVS flash.
  5. Built-in Safety: Automated shutoff engages if optics reach 31.0°C or if Abort Tuning is clicked.
